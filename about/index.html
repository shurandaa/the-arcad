A closer look at the design decisions behind this arcade.

The mini crossword

A word square uses the same five answers across and down, giving this small puzzle a deliberate symmetry.

Playing without scripts

Each square is a native text field. Players enter one character at a time and compare their work with the solution.

Keyboard navigation

Tab moves through the open squares in reading order. Shift + Tab moves backward.

Checking an answer

After the final square, Tab reaches “Reveal solution.” Press Enter or Space to open it, then read the numbered answer grid and answer lists.
